Privacy notice

MountainNotes web app, beta · as of 14 November 2026

Who is responsible

MountainNotes is a student project by Gian Schneider (module Advanced Spatial Methods, FHNW, Switzerland). It is a beta for invited testers, not a commercial service. Questions, requests for information or deletion: github.com/gianschneider/mountainnotes-web/issues.

In short

  • Your position and your routes are never stored on the server. Whether you stand at a note is checked on your phone.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no cookies.
  • You can delete your account with all your notes at any time in the app (Profile → Delete account).

What the server stores

  • Account: username and password (only as a secure hash, never in plain text).
  • Your notes: text, optional photo, the location of the note, validity period and who may read it. Photos are scaled down and all metadata (EXIF: GPS position, time, camera) is removed on your phone before upload.
  • Groups: groups you create or join, and join requests.
  • Openings: which note you opened and when. The author of a note can see who opened it.

What the server sees but does not keep

To load the notes around you, the app asks the server for an area of about 40 × 40 km around your position, rounded to about 1 km. Like every web server, the server writes a technical log (time, IP address, requested address). The log is not evaluated and is overwritten automatically when it is full.

On your phone

Notes meant for you, notes waiting for upload and photos are stored in your browser so that the app works offline. The content is encrypted with a key that never leaves your device. Saved map areas are stored unencrypted (public map data).

Other services

  • Map: map tiles come from swisstopo (Federal Office of Topography, vectortiles.geo.admin.ch). swisstopo sees your IP address and the map area you look at, as with any web map.
  • Hosting: the server is a Raspberry Pi in Switzerland. It is reached through Tailscale Funnel (Tailscale Inc., USA); the connection is encrypted up to the Pi, so Tailscale cannot read it.

How long data is kept

Notes disappear after their end date. Your account and notes stay until you delete them, or until the beta ends. There is no backup: during the beta, data can be lost.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) you can ask what data is stored about you, have it corrected or deleted. Most of it you can do yourself in the app; for anything else, use the contact above.
